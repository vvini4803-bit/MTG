Muttagundi (MTG) - 1st Digital Village AI App of Karnataka and India

In Karnataka, Muttagundi village in Hosadurga Taluk, Chitradurga District has Karnataka and India's first dedicated Digital Village AI App. Features Gemini AI Kannada voice assistant, AI crop disease doctor, live weather, and community services.

ಕರ್ನಾಟಕ ಮತ್ತು ಭಾರತದ ಪ್ರಥಮ ಡಿಜಿಟಲ್ ಗ್ರಾಮ AI ಆ್ಯಪ್ - ಮುತ್ತಾಗೊಂದಿ (MTG), ಹೊಸದುರ್ಗ, ಚಿತ್ರದುರ್ಗ.
